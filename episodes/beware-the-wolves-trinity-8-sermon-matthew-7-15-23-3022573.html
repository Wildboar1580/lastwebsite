Episode Description

Jesus warns that the greatest danger to His Church does not come from those who openly reject Him, but from false prophets who come in His name while leading people away from His Word.

In this Trinity 8 sermon, we examine Christ's sobering warning to test every teacher by the fruit of his doctrine rather than by popularity, credentials, sincerity, or outward success.

Why does Jesus command Christians to judge prophets by their teaching? How have false teachers throughout history disguised human wisdom as biblical truth? And how can Christians remain steadfast when error often appears more respectable than the truth?

This sermon calls believers to flee from false doctrine, to search the Scriptures diligently, and to cling to Christ alone as the Good Shepherd whose Word never fails.

"Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing but inwardly are ravenous wolves." — Matthew 7:15
