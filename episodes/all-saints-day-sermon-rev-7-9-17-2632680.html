Episode Description

This All Saints’ Day sermon reminds us that the Church is one in Christ — the saints who rest in Him and the faithful who still journey on earth. Rooted in Scripture and the Book of Concord, it proclaims our hope in the Lamb who conquered death and gathers His people from every nation. In Christ alone, we share the victory that no grave can take away.
