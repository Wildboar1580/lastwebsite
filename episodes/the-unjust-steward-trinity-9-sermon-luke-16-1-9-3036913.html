Episode Description

Podcast Title The Unjust Steward — Trinity 9 Sermon (Luke 16:1–9)

Why would Jesus commend a dishonest steward? In one of His most surprising parables, Christ is not praising deception but exposing how seriously the unbelieving world pursues temporary security while Christians often treat the promises of eternal life as though they were of little importance.

In this Trinity 9 sermon, we explore Jesus' call to live with genuine faith in His Word. The unjust steward recognized that judgment was coming and acted decisively. Christ asks why the children of this world often show greater urgency for passing earthly gain than the children of God show for the eternal kingdom.

This sermon confronts the false security of culturally acceptable Christianity, the temptation to excuse cherished sins, and the danger of trusting in wealth, institutions, or human schemes rather than in Christ alone. It also proclaims the sure hope of the Gospel: Jesus has won for us an everlasting inheritance, an eternal Sabbath rest, and communion with God that far surpass every treasure of this passing world.

If Christ's promises are true—and they are—then nothing in this life is worth more than hearing His Word, clinging to His Gospel, and trusting Him alone for salvation.

"Make friends for yourselves by means of unrighteous mammon, so that when it fails, they may receive you into the eternal dwellings." — Luke 16:9
