Representative Text

163. While with ceaseless course the sun

Text and metadata are organized from Hymnary’s ELHB index and representative text pages.

1. While with ceaseless course the sun

Hasted thru' the former year,

Many souls their race have run,

Never more to meet us here:

Fixed in an eternal state,

They have done with all below;

We a little longer wait,

But how little, none can know.

2. As the winged arrow flies

Speedily the mark to find,

As the ligh'tning from the skies

Darts and leaves no trace behind;

Swiftly thus our fleeting days

Bear us down life’s rapid stream;

Upward, Lord, our spirits raise;

All below is but a dream.

3. Thanks for mercies past receive;

Pardon of our sins renew;

Teach us henceforth how to live,

With eternity in view.

Bless thy word to young and old,

Fill us with a Savior's love;

And when life’s short tale is told,

May we dwell with thee above.

Source: Hymns and Devotions for Daily Worship #31
