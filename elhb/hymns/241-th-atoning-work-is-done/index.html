Representative Text

241. Th' atoning work is done

Text and metadata are organized from Hymnary’s ELHB index and representative text pages.

1 Th'atoning work is done,

The Victim’s blood is shed,

And Jesus now is gone

His people’s cause to plead:

He stands in heaven their great High Priest,

And bears their names upon His breast.

2 He sprinkled with His blood

The mercy-seat above;

For justice had withstood

The purposes of love;

But justice now withstands no more,

And mercy yields her boundless store.

3 No temple made with hands

His place of service is;

In heaven itself He stands,

A heavenly priesthood His:

In Him the shadows of the Law

Are all fulfilled, and now withdraw.

4 And though awhile He be

Hid from the eyes of men,

His people look to see

Their great High Priest again:

In brightest glory He will come,

And take His waiting people home.
