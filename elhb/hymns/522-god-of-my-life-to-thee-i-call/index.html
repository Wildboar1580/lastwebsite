Representative Text

522. God of my life, to Thee I call

Text and metadata are organized from Hymnary’s ELHB index and representative text pages.

1 God of our life, to thee we call,
afflicted at thy feet we fall;
when the great water-floods prevail,
leave not our trembling hearts to fail.
2 Friend of the friendless and the saint,
Where should we lodge our deep complaint?
Where but with thee, whose open door
invites the helpless and the poor?
3 Did ever mourner plead with thee,
and thou refuse that mourner’s plea?
Does not the Word still fixed remain,
that none shall seek thy face in vain?

Source noted on Hymnary: CPWI Hymnal #468
