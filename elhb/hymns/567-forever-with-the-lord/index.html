ELHB Hymn 567

"Forever with the Lord!"

James Montgomery (1835)

View on Hymnary Open page scan

Representative Text

567. "Forever with the Lord!"

Text and metadata are organized from Hymnary’s ELHB index and representative text pages.

1 "Forever with the Lord!"

Amen, so let it be!

Life from the dead is in that word,

'Tis immortality.

2 Here in the body pent

Absent from Him I roam,

Yet nightly pitch my moving tent

A days march nearer home.

3 "Forever with the Lord!"

Father if 'tis Thy will,

The promise of that faithful word,

E'en here to me fulfill.

4 So when my latest breath

Shall rend the vail in twain,

By death I shall escape from death,

And life eternal gain.

5 Knowing as I am known,

How shall I love that word?

And oft repeat before the throne,

"Forever with the Lord!"

Source: African Methodist Episcopal Church Hymnal #477
