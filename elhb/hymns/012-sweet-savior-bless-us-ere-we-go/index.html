Hymn Text

12. Sweet Savior, bless us ere we go

Text and metadata are organized from Hymnary’s ELHB index pages and ELHL1918 instance pages.

1 Sweet Savior, bless us ere we go;
Thy Word into our minds instil;
And make our luke-warm hearts to glow
With lowly love and fervent will.
Refrain:
Through life’s long day and death’s dark night,
O gentle Jesus, be our light.
2 The day is gone, its hours have run,
And Thou hast taken count of all,
The scanty triumphs grace hath won,
The broken vow, the frequent fall. [Refrain]
3 Grant us, dear Lord, from evil ways
True absolution and release,
And bless us, more than in past days,
With purity and inward peace. [Refrain]
4 For all we love, the poor, the sad,
The sinful, unto Thee we call;
O let Thy mercy make us glad;
Thou art our Savior, and our all. [Refrain]

Source noted on Hymnary: Evangelical Lutheran Hymn-book #12
