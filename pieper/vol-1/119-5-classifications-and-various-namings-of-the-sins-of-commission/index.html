5. Classifications and various namings of the sins of commission.

The manifold divisions of the peccata actualia, if they have ground in Scripture, are not to be considered useless or "boring." Rather, as Chemnitz points out, they serve to ward off1646) carnal security, in that Christians see how many wild beasts slumber in their own flesh and are to be fought by them. Unusquisque igitur verorum Christianorum una cum Paulo, 1 Cor. 9:27, proprium suum corpus contundat et in servitutem redigat, ne reprobus fiat.
