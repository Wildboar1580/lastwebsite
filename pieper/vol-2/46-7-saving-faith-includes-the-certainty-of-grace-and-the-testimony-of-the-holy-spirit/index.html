7. Saving Faith Includes the Certainty of Grace and the Testimony of the Holy Spirit.

Saving faith includes certainty of grace because it rests on God's own promise in the Gospel. The Holy Spirit creates this certainty through the Word, so that confidence in forgiveness belongs to faith itself and is not an optional addition for especially advanced Christians.
