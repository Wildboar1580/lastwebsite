Of course, Scripture also says that man is converted from the doing of sin to the keeping of the statutes of God. 7’ But in these scriptural statements conversion is described according to the fruits inseparably connected with conversion of the heart, and through which conversion of the heart must become evident to people who cannot see into the heart. The conversion of man is primo loco, in the true and narrow sense, conversion to the Gospel or to Christ, the Savior of sinners. (Acts 11:20-21; 1 Petr. 2:26.)
