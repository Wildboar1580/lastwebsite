The Essence of Christianity

A dedicated work page for Pieper's essay from The Essence of Christianity: Seven Convention Essays, gathered so it can sit beside the rest of your Pieper library.

Return to Pieper's Works or use the links below to read the source text and listen to the episode reading.

Work Summary

Christianity defined by Christ, not by human morality

This essay opens the seven-essay collection by asking what makes Christianity distinct from every other religion. Pieper's answer centers not on man's efforts, but on Christ's saving work for sinners.

Source document: The Essence of Christianity: Seven Convention Essays, compiled by Back to Luther in 2025.
