June 2026 Giving Report

School help reached children in Uganda, and the need remains urgent.

In June 2026, gifts for school support were distributed in Uganda. Because the available funds were limited, we were only able to sponsor one child's tuition, but we were also able to provide school supplies for many more children.

Video Report

Gratitude for what was given, prayer for what remains

The video shows the June 2026 distribution of school help in Uganda. Children received supplies that can be used right away, and one child's tuition was sponsored from the funds available.

We are thankful for every gift, because even a small distribution matters to a child and family facing school expenses. At the same time, this report shows why continued giving is needed. The need is larger than one distribution, and many more children still need tuition and supplies.

Future gifts will help us expand beyond this first tuition sponsorship so more children can return to school with dignity, readiness, and hope.
