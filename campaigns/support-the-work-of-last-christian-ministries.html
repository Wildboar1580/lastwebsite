Support the Podcast and Local Ministry

This is not the Uganda medical clinic fund. This page is for the preaching, podcast, writing, teaching, and local ministry work being carried out in Michigan through Last Christian Ministries.

What Makes This Different

The medical clinic remains the site’s main featured fundraiser because it is a specific mercy project in Uganda. This page is different. It supports the core ministry work behind Last Christian Ministries here in Michigan.

That includes recording and publishing podcast episodes, writing and teaching biblical and Lutheran material, maintaining the website and library, and supporting the ordinary work of preaching and local ministry. It is the right fund for people who want to support the ministry itself rather than the Uganda clinic campaign.

Gifts to this fund help make the podcast sustainable and help support the work that goes into preparing sermons, recording episodes, publishing resources, and keeping the ministry’s teaching available online. It also helps create margin for local ministry efforts, pastoral work, and the practical costs involved in keeping the ministry active and public.

If your goal is to support the preaching and teaching work directly, this is the best page to use. If your goal is specifically the clinic in Uganda, the clinic campaign is still the main featured fundraiser on the site.
