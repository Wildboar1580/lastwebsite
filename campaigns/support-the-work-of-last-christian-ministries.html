Why This Fund Matters

The medical clinic remains the site’s main featured fundraiser because it is a concrete long-term project. But the ministry in Uganda also has daily needs that do not fit neatly into one building campaign. Families still need help before a clinic is built, church programs still need support, and vulnerable households still need care when hunger, sickness, and poverty press in.

This general fund supports the broader mercy work surrounding the ministry. It helps sustain encouragement in the Scriptures, church attendance and fellowship, care for orphans and elderly people, help for disabled people, and practical support when households have nowhere else to turn.

Kutesa Henry has been serving this community by encouraging people to read the Bible, attend church, and remain steadfast in Christian life while also caring for physical needs. Earlier efforts were supported in part by agriculture, but as the land lost fertility and the crops failed, that support became less dependable even while community need remained high.

This fund gives flexibility for the work that continues every week: visiting households, helping people stay connected to the church, assisting vulnerable children, standing with widows and elderly people, and responding to needs that arise outside a single named project.
