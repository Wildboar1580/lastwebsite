About This Collection

Walther's Gospel Sermons

This section gathers the two Year of Grace volumes into individual sermon pages so you can move through Walther's Gospel preaching from Advent to the later Sundays after Trinity.

Sources

Two translated Year of Grace volumes

These pages follow the published Google Doc texts linked from the two Back to Luther Year of Grace posts.

Coverage

62 sermon pages

Read across the church year from Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, Pentecost, and deep into Trinity season.

Use

Built for ordinary reading

Each sermon has its own page with previous and next links so the whole collection can be browsed in sequence.
