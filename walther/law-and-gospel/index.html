This page is ready to become the landing point for Walther's The Proper Distinction Between Law and Gospel. A strong next step would be to organize the work by evening lectures so readers can move through it sequentially.

As you build it out, this page can hold a short introduction, a table of contents for the lectures, and a search index that highlights themes like repentance, consolation, preaching, absolution, and the comfort of terrified consciences.
