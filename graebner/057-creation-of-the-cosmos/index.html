Graebner Text

57 - Creation of the Cosmos

CREATION OF THE COSMOS

 § 57. The creation of the inanimate and irrational world was begun and completed by the Triune God1 within six consecutive days2 by the exertion and for the manifestation and glory of His power,3 wisdom,4 and goodness.5

1. The creation of the inanimate and irrational world was begun and completed by the Triune God

Genesis 1:1 In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.

Psalm 33:6 By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of His mouth.

Colossians 1:15, 16 Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: for by Him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible.

See the record of creation Genesis 1 and Genesis 2.

2. within six consecutive days

Genesis 1:5 And the evening and the morning were the first day. Cf. v. 8. 13. 19. 23. 31.

Exodus 20:9, 11 Six days shalt thou labor, and do all thy work. (11) For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is.

Exodus 31:17 In six days the Lord made heaven and earth.

3. by the exertion and for the manifestation and glory of His power,

Jeremiah 32:17 O Lord God! behold, Thou hast made the heaven and the earth by Thy great power.

4. wisdom,

Psalm 104:24 O Lord, how manifold are Thy works! In wisdom hast Thou made them all.

Psalm 136:5 To Him that by wisdom made the heavens: for His mercy endureth for ever.

5. and goodness.

Psalm 136:5 To Him that by wisdom made the heavens: for His mercy endureth for ever.

Revelation 4:11 Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honor and power: for Thou hast created all things, and for Thy pleasure they are and were created.
