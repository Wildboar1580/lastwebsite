Graebner Text

100 - Unity of Person

UNITY OF PERSON § 100. Though two complete and distinct natures are united in Christ,1 there is in Him no union of two persons,2 since His human nature at no time subsisted by itself,3 but only in personal union, not with the Father,4 nor with the Holy Ghost,5 but with God the Son, the second Person in the Trinity.6

1. Though two complete and distinct natures are united in Christ,

Cf.§ 99.

2. there is in Him no union of two persons,

Luke 1:35 The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that Holy Thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God.

I Timothy 2:5 There is one God, and one Mediator … the Man Christ Jesus.

3. since His human nature at no time subsisted by itself,

  John 1:14 And the Word was made flesh.

  Luke 1:35. See above sub 2.

4. but only in personal union, not with the Father,

  John 17:5 And now, O Father, glorify Thou Me with Thine own self with the glory which I had with Thee before the world was.

  I John 1:7 But if we walk in the light, as He is in the light, we have fellowship one with another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanseth us from all sin.

  John 5:21, 27 As the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom He will. (27) And hath given Him authority to execute judgment also, because He is the Son of Man.

5. nor with the Holy Ghost,

  Matthew 12:28 But if I cast out devils by the Spirit of God. …

  John 15:26 But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, He shall testify of Me.

6. but with God the Son, the second Person in the Trinity.

Texts sub 4 and 5.
